Meat Sauce:
▪Olive oil
▪450g (1 lb) 80/20 ground chuck
▪225g (8 oz) hot Italian pork sausage, casings removed 
▪200g (1 med) yellow onion, grated
▪20g (5 cloves) garlic, minced or pressed
▪Coarse salt, to taste
▪75g ( ⅓ c) tomato paste
▪1g (½ t) red chili flake
▪1g (½ t) dried oregano
▪800g (28 oz can) crushed tomatoes
▪550g (2⅓ c) water
▪15g (1 T) beef Better Than Bouillon
▪7 to 8g (2 t) granulated sugar
▪10g (4 Tbsp) fresh basil, chopped

Ricotta Mixture:
▪450g (1 lb) whole milk ricotta
▪50g (1/2 c) Parmesan, finely grated
▪5g (1 t) salt
▪2g (1 t) black pepper
▪1 egg

Pasta and Cheese:
▪225g (8 oz) oven-ready lasagna noodles
▪150g (about 1⅓ c) shredded mozz
▪1/2c grated Parmesan for topping

1 Preheat oven to 400°F/205C. Break the noodles into roughly 2-inch pieces by Grate onions, mince garlic, chop basil.

2 Heat Dutch oven over medium-high with a squiggle of olive oil. Add the ground beef and sausage and brown, breaking the meat down until fully cooked and about the size of lentils, around 8 minutes

3 Stir in the onion, garlic and pinch of salt. Cook 3-5 min until the onion is softened

4 Add tomato paste. Cook, stirring constantly, until the tomato paste darkens to a rusty brick red. 

5 Add the crushed tomatoes, water, chili flake, oregano, BTB and sugar. scrap up any browned bits from the bottom. Bring to  simmer, cover partially and cook over low heat 3 to 5 minutes.

6 Mix the ricotta, Parmesan, salt, black pepper and egg until smooth.
Of heat, taste and adjust with pinch of salt and/or sugar if needed. Stir in basil

7 Scatter ⅓ of the noodles over the sauce in an even layer and press them down until fully submerged. Repeat two more times pressing each layer beneath the sauce before adding the next. On the final layer, push sauce up and over any exposed noodles.

8 Spoon the ricotta mixture over the top in 12 to 15 evenly spaced dollops

9 Top with the mozzarella and Parm

10 Wipe any sauce off the inside rim of the pot with a damp towel

11 Cover and bake at 400°F/205C for 15 minutes.

12 Increase oven temp to 450°F/230C, remove lid and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until the cheese is deeply browned in spots, edges are bubbling and a knife slides through the noodles with no resistance.

13 Rest 20 minutes
